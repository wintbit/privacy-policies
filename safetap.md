## 1. Overview
Safe-Tap is an enterprise-grade tactical emergency distress dispatch and tamper-evident forensic evidence sentinel. We respect your digital privacy. We do not sell your personal data, nor do we track your activities across external third-party apps for advertising purposes.

## 2. Information We Collect
To provide reliable functionality for lone workers, first responders, vulnerable individuals, security professionals, and everyday users seeking discreet personal safety, Safe-Tap collects and processes:

* **Precise Geolocation (GPS):** Acquired strictly upon user tap trigger to transmit immediate emergency coordinates to designated emergency contacts
* **Ambient Audio Telemetry:** 5-second acoustic evidence recorded during armed distress dispatches and cryptographically hashed with SHA-256 for courtroom proof
* **Emergency Contacts:** Contact names and phone numbers stored in encrypted device sandboxes to route automated distress dispatches
* **First Responder Medical ID:** Blood type, critical allergies, and emergency medical notes stored locally on-device

## 3. Data Storage & Security
* **On-Device Storage:** Local data is saved securely within sandboxed application storage.
* **Cloud Infrastructure:** Data in transit is protected using encrypted TLS 1.3 connections, with cloud databases secured by authentication controls and access security rules.

## 4. Third-Party Services
Safe-Tap integrates strictly with trusted enterprise infrastructure providers:

* **Cellular SMS Infrastructure:** Carrier SMS gateways for local offline emergency alert transmission
* **Multiplatform Device Keystores:** Android Keystore and Apple Secure Enclave for cryptographic salt and evidence seal generation

## 5. Account Deletion & Data Erasure Instructions
In accordance with global privacy standards (GDPR, CCPA, and Apple App Store / Google Play requirements), users have the right to delete their account and wipe all stored data:

* **Device Data Wipe:** Clear all emergency contacts, custom tap cadences, and forensic evidence seals at any time via Settings > Reset Security Controls or by uninstalling the app
* **Cryptographic Purge:** Master PIN and encrypted preferences are permanently erased immediately upon reset
* **Support Inquiries:** Contact wintbit.dev@gmail.com with subject line 'Safe-Tap Data Erasure Request'

## 6. Operational Rules, Restrictions & Immutability
* **Zero Tracking Ad Policy:** Safe-Tap never sells, rents, tracks, or shares user data with third-party ad networks
* **Offline-First Architecture:** Emergency alerts and evidence records are processed locally on the device without mandatory cloud upload
* **Cryptographic Chain of Custody:** All emergency dispatch manifests and audio records are sealed with tamper-evident digital signatures

## 7. Children's Privacy
Safe-Tap is designed for general personal safety and lone-worker protection. Children under 13 must only use Safe-Tap under the supervision of a parent, legal guardian, or designated emergency contact.
