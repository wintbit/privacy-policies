## 1. Overview
PardnrLedger is a cryptographic communal finance management, lottery syndicate governance, and escrow verification application. We respect your digital privacy. We do not sell your personal data, nor do we track your activities across external third-party apps for advertising purposes.

## 2. Information We Collect
To provide reliable functionality for syndicate managers, communal savings participants, and pool members, PardnrLedger collects and processes:

* **Device Identifiers & Public Keys:** Cryptographic public keys generated in hardware keystores (Android Keystore / iOS Secure Enclave) for transaction signing and account identity
* **Camera & Photo Gallery:** Used exclusively for scanning member invitation QR codes and uploading drawing ticket/settlement evidence receipts
* **Local Encrypted Ledger & Transaction History:** Pool configurations, member contribution records, and payout distributions stored in local encrypted databases
* **Off-Chain Artifact Metadata:** IPFS Content Identifiers (CIDs) and SHA-256 cryptographic hashes for tamper-evident ticket auditing

## 3. Data Storage & Security
* **On-Device Storage:** Local data is saved securely within sandboxed application storage.
* **Cloud Infrastructure:** Data in transit is protected using encrypted TLS 1.3 connections, with cloud databases secured by authentication controls and access security rules.

## 4. Third-Party Services
PardnrLedger integrates strictly with trusted enterprise infrastructure providers:

* **XRPL (XRP Ledger):** Public decentralized ledger for cryptographic anchoring and multi-sig escrow verification
* **IPFS (Pinata):** Decentralized encrypted artifact and evidence pinning
* **Cloudflare Workers & Stripe:** Serverless backend for optional Host Pro and Enterprise license verification
* **Apple TestFlight & Google Play:** Beta testing and application distribution

## 5. Account Deletion & Data Erasure Instructions
In accordance with global privacy standards (GDPR, CCPA, and Apple App Store / Google Play requirements), users have the right to delete their account and wipe all stored data:

* **Local Vault Wipe:** Reset your local application state or uninstall the app to instantly destroy your local cryptographic keypairs and transaction cache
* **Off-Chain Purge:** Email wintbit.dev@gmail.com to delete auxiliary support records and license metadata
* **Ledger Immutability Disclosure:** Public cryptographic ledger entries previously broadcast to XRPL or IPFS cannot be modified or deleted due to decentralized blockchain immutability

## 6. Operational Rules, Restrictions & Immutability
* **Decentralized Immutability:** Transactions and cryptographic proofs committed to public decentralized networks (XRPL / IPFS) are permanent and mathematically immutable
* **Non-Custodial Architecture:** We never possess or store your private cryptographic keys
* **Zero Cross-App Tracking:** We do not employ third-party advertising identifiers or behavioral trackers

## 7. Children's Privacy
PardnrLedger is intended for adult syndicate organizers and communal finance participants. It is not intended for use by individuals under the age of 18 (or the legal age of majority in your jurisdiction).
