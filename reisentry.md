## 1. Overview
ReiSentry is an enterprise construction milestone verification and real estate investment governance platform. We respect your digital privacy. We do not sell your personal data, nor do we track your activities across external third-party apps for advertising purposes.

## 2. Information We Collect
To provide reliable functionality for real estate investors, project managers, contractors, and inspectors, ReiSentry collects and processes:

* **Location Data (GPS Coordinates):** Collected only during user-initiated actions to geo-tag construction progress photos on parcel boundaries and verify job site attendance
* **Camera & Media Library:** Used to photograph and attach construction progress evidence (before, during, after) and quality control inspection checklists
* **User Profiles & Stakeholder Roles:** Professional email, full name, phone number, and stakeholder role (General Contractor, Subcontractor, Project Manager, Inspector, Investor)
* **Operational & Financial Records:** Scope of Work (SOW) milestone progress, draw disbursement requests, and audit verification sign-offs

## 3. Data Storage & Security
* **On-Device Storage:** Local data is saved securely within sandboxed application storage.
* **Cloud Infrastructure:** Data in transit is protected using encrypted TLS 1.3 connections, with cloud databases secured by authentication controls and access security rules.

## 4. Third-Party Services
ReiSentry integrates strictly with trusted enterprise infrastructure providers:

* **Supabase:** Encrypted PostgreSQL database hosting, authentication, and evidence storage
* **Apple TestFlight:** Beta build distribution, crash analytics, and tester feedback

## 5. Children's Privacy
ReiSentry is an enterprise B2B platform designed for construction professionals and real estate investors. It is not intended for use by individuals under the age of 18.

## 6. Account & Data Deletion
Users can request full deletion of their account and associated data by emailing **wintbit.dev@gmail.com** with the subject *"Data Deletion Request"*.
