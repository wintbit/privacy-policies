## 1. Overview
ReiSentry is an enterprise real estate investment governance and construction milestone verification platform. We do not sell your personal data, nor do we use your data for advertising tracking.

## 2. Information We Collect
* **Location Data (GPS Coordinates):** Collected only while using the application to geo-tag field evidence photos and verify on-site attendance.
* **Camera & Photo Library:** Used to capture and attach construction evidence, defect logs, and inspection documentation.
* **User Profiles & Role Credentials:** Professional email, name, phone, and role (General Contractor, Subcontractor, Project Manager, Inspector, Investor).
* **Project & Financial Milestones:** Scope of Work progress, draw requests, and disbursement records.

## 3. Data Storage & Security
* **On-Device Storage:** Local data is saved within a secure, sandboxed AndroidX Room SQLite database.
* **Cloud Infrastructure:** Remote sync is powered by encrypted TLS 1.3 connections to Supabase PostgreSQL with strict Row-Level Security (RLS) policies.

## 4. Account & Data Deletion
Users can request full deletion of their account and associated data by emailing **support@reilogic.com** with the subject *"Data Deletion Request"*.
