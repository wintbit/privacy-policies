ReiSentry Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 02, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how ReiSentry ("we", "our", or "the App") collects, uses, stores, and protects information when you use our mobile and cloud applications.

Our Privacy Promise: ReiSentry is an enterprise construction verification and real estate investment governance platform. We do not sell your personal information, nor do we use your data for advertising tracking. All collected data serves strictly to verify job site milestones, manage construction draws, and maintain transparent audit trails.

1. Information We Collect

A. Location Data (GPS Coordinates)

ReiSentry collects approximate and precise location data when you interact with specific on-site features:

  • Evidence Geo-Tagging: Capturing longitude and latitude to verify that construction progress photos and inspection reports were recorded on the physical parcel boundaries.
  • Geofenced Attendance: Validating field personnel clock-in/out events relative to project boundaries.

Note: Location coordinates are captured only at the time of user-initiated actions and are not tracked in the background when the app is closed.

B. Camera, Media & Files

We request access to your device camera and photo library strictly to allow users to:

  • Photograph and record progress evidence (before, during, and after milestone execution).
  • Upload engineering inspection checklists and architectural documents.
  • Attach punch list and defect verification media to specific Scope of Work (SOW) tickets.

C. Account & Identity Credentials

To facilitate multi-party stakeholder collaboration, we collect:

  • User profile details (Full Name, Professional Email Address, Phone Number).
  • Enterprise stakeholder role (e.g., General Contractor, Subcontractor, Project Manager, Inspector, Investor/Owner).
  • Authentication tokens securely managed via encrypted session storage.

D. Operational & Financial Governance Records

To enable real estate investment controls and draw disbursements:

  • Scope of Work milestones, line-item completion percentages, and daily work logs.
  • Financial draw requests, milestone disallowances, and inspector verification sign-offs.
  • Cryptographic SHA-256 evidence integrity hashes and audit verification logs.

2. How We Use Collected Information

  • Milestone Verification: Corroborating physical work with time-stamped, geo-verified photo/video evidence.
  • Draw & Disbursement Governance: Providing investors and project managers with verified evidence before funds are disbursed.
  • Audit Trails: Maintaining non-repudiation records for contractor tasks, inspector approvals, and quality control compliance.
  • Sync & Offline Availability: Storing local data in Room database and synchronizing with Supabase PostgreSQL cloud infrastructure.

3. Data Storage, Security & Retention

🔒 Enterprise Security Architecture: All data in transit is encrypted using industry-standard TLS 1.3. Local device data is stored in sandboxed SQLite databases with optional AES-256 encryption. Cloud data resides on Supabase PostgreSQL servers protected by Row-Level Security (RLS) policies.

We retain operational records for the duration of active project lifecycles and compliance retention requirements mandated by construction real estate governance.

4. Third-Party Services & Infrastructure

ReiSentry integrates with trusted enterprise infrastructure providers:

  • Supabase (PostgreSQL & Storage): Cloud database hosting, auth session verification, and secure evidence media storage.
  • Apple TestFlight: Beta build distribution, crash analytics, and tester feedback.

We do not integrate with third-party advertising SDKs, tracking pixels, or data brokers.

5. User Rights & Data Deletion

Depending on your jurisdiction (such as GDPR or CCPA), you have the right to access, export, rectify, or request the deletion of your personal account data.

To request data deletion, contact us at wintbit.dev@gmail.com with the subject "Data Deletion Request". Requests are processed within 30 days.

6. Children's Privacy

ReiSentry is an enterprise B2B platform designed for construction professionals and real estate investors. It is not intended for use by individuals under the age of 18.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy periodically. Any updates will be posted to this page with an updated effective date. Continued use of the application following changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
