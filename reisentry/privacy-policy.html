ReiSentry Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 02, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how ReiSentry ("we", "our", or "the App") collects, uses, stores, and protects information when you use our mobile and cloud applications.

Our Privacy Promise: ReiSentry is an enterprise construction verification and real estate investment governance platform. We do not sell your personal information, nor do we use your data for advertising tracking. All collected data serves strictly to verify job site milestones, manage construction draws, and maintain transparent audit trails.

1. Information We Collect

A. Location Data (GPS Coordinates)

ReiSentry collects approximate and precise location data when you interact with specific on-site features:

  • Evidence Geo-Tagging: Capturing longitude and latitude to verify that construction progress photos and inspection reports were recorded on the physical parcel boundaries.
  • Geofenced Attendance: Validating field personnel clock-in/out events relative to project boundaries.

Location access is requested only while using the application and is never tracked continuously in the background.

B. Camera & Photos / Media Library

  • Field Evidence Photos: Access to your device camera to capture Before, During, and After milestone images.
  • Document Attachments: Access to selected gallery photos for uploading receipts, architectural plans, and signed inspection permits.

C. User Profile & Authentication Information

  • Full Name, professional email address, phone number, and company/trade affiliation.
  • Role assignment (e.g., General Contractor, Subcontractor, Project Manager, Inspector, Investor, Administrator) for cryptographic permission verification.

D. Project & Milestone Data

  • Scope of Work (SOW) items, task progress percentages, daily work logs, inspection notes, draw disbursement requests, and currency conversion preferences.

2. How We Use Your Information

The information collected is used exclusively for:

  • Providing real-time progress synchronization across stakeholder devices.
  • Preventing milestone fraud through geo-stamped and time-stamped proof of work.
  • Authorizing and auditing construction draw disbursements.
  • Enabling offline-first local caching so field workers can log progress without cellular connectivity.

3. Data Storage & Security

  • Local Device Storage: App data is stored in a private, sandboxed AndroidX Room SQLite database on your device.
  • Cloud Infrastructure: Remote synchronization uses encrypted HTTPS/TLS 1.3 connections to enterprise Supabase PostgreSQL infrastructure with strict Row-Level Security (RLS) policies.
  • Role-Based Access: Access to financial disbursements and sensitive milestone approvals is strictly restricted according to authenticated stakeholder roles.

4. Data Sharing & Third-Party Services

We do not share, sell, or rent your personal information to third parties. Data is shared only within authorized project workspaces among designated stakeholders (e.g., project managers, property owners, and verified inspectors).

We utilize the following infrastructure service providers:

  • Supabase Cloud: Encrypted database hosting and file storage under enterprise security standards.

5. Data Retention & Account Deletion

We retain project records for the duration of the construction lifecycle and audit retention compliance. You have the right to request deletion of your account and personal data at any time.

To request data deletion, contact us at wintbit.dev@gmail.com with the subject "Data Deletion Request". Requests are processed within 30 days.

6. Children's Privacy

ReiSentry is an enterprise business application designed for working professionals aged 18 and older. We do not knowingly collect personal data from individuals under 18 years of age.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy periodically. Any updates will be posted to this page with an updated effective date. Continued use of the application following changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
