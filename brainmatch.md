## 1. Overview
BrainMatch is an interactive memory and cognitive skill-building game. We respect your digital privacy. We do not sell your personal data, nor do we track your activities across external third-party apps for advertising purposes.

## 2. Information We Collect
To provide reliable functionality for students and players of all ages, BrainMatch collects and processes:

* **Local Gameplay & Memory Progress:** Scores, completed levels, best times, and achievement badges stored locally in sandboxed storage
* **Game Settings & Accessibility:** Sound effects volume, music volume, speech toggles, visual hints, and parental settings stored locally
* **Camera Access (Settings Sync):** Camera used exclusively for on-device scanning of QR codes for peer-to-peer settings synchronization without cloud servers
* **In-App Purchases (StoreKit):** In-app purchase entitlement verification handled directly by Apple StoreKit

## 3. Data Storage & Security
* **On-Device Storage:** Local data is saved securely within sandboxed application storage.
* **Cloud Infrastructure:** Data in transit is protected using encrypted TLS 1.3 connections, with cloud databases secured by authentication controls and access security rules.

## 4. Third-Party Services
BrainMatch integrates strictly with trusted enterprise infrastructure providers:

* **Apple App Store:** Secure application updates, family sharing, and distribution
* **Apple StoreKit:** In-app purchase entitlement verification for premium unlock

## 5. Account Deletion & Data Erasure Instructions
In accordance with global privacy standards (GDPR, CCPA, and Apple App Store / Google Play requirements), users have the right to delete their account and wipe all stored data:

* **Local Progress Reset:** Clear local memory game progress, best times, and achievements by clearing application storage or uninstalling the app
* **Instant Erasure:** Because all data is stored strictly on-device, removing the app permanently erases all records immediately
* **Support Correspondence Erasure:** Email wintbit.dev@gmail.com with subject 'Data Deletion Request' to purge any email support inquiries

## 6. Operational Rules, Restrictions & Immutability
* **No Behavioral Tracking:** We do not track player activities across third-party apps or websites
* **Zero Data Brokerage:** We never sell or monetize user data
* **On-Device Gameplay:** Game logic and memory puzzles run completely locally on your device

## 7. Children's Privacy
BrainMatch complies with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), GDPR-K, and Apple Kids Category policies. We do not collect, transmit, share, or monetize any personal identifying information from children under 13.
