## 1. Overview
BrainMatch is an interactive memory and cognitive skill-building game. We respect your digital privacy. We do not sell your personal data, nor do we track your activities across external third-party apps for advertising purposes.

## 2. Information We Collect
To provide reliable functionality for students and players of all ages, BrainMatch collects and processes:

* **Local Game Progress:** Scores, completed levels, and achievement badges stored locally on your device
* **Gameplay Diagnostics:** Anonymous crash and performance metrics to ensure smooth puzzle execution

## 3. Data Storage & Security
* **On-Device Storage:** Local data is saved securely within sandboxed application storage.
* **Cloud Infrastructure:** Data in transit is protected using encrypted TLS 1.3 connections, with cloud databases secured by authentication controls and access security rules.

## 4. Third-Party Services
BrainMatch integrates strictly with trusted enterprise infrastructure providers:

* **Apple Game Center:** Optional leaderboards and achievement synchronization
* **App Store:** Secure application updates and distribution

## 5. Children's Privacy
BrainMatch complies with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA). We do not collect, store, or share any personal identifying information from children under 13.

## 6. Account & Data Deletion
Users can request full deletion of their account and associated data by emailing **wintbit.dev@gmail.com** with the subject *"Data Deletion Request"*.
