Little Brain Learners Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 19, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Little Brain Learners ("we", "our", or "the App") collects, uses, stores, and protects information when you interact with our applications and services.

Our Privacy Commitment: Little Brain Learners is an early childhood learning and foundational literacy application. We respect your digital privacy. We do not sell your personal data, nor do we track your activities across external third-party apps for advertising purposes.

1. Information We Collect

To provide reliable and secure functionality for young learners, parents, and educators, Little Brain Learners collects and processes the following information:

  • Educational & Gameplay Progress: Offline lesson completion records, shape/color/word accuracy, memory puzzle times, achievement stars, and daily streaks stored strictly in local device storage
  • Parental Settings & Accessibility: Sound volume controls, TTS speech toggles, visual hints, high-contrast mode, screen time limits, and parental gate preferences stored locally
  • Custom Family Memory Decks (Optional): Family member names, relations, and local photos chosen by parents for personalized memory cards, stored exclusively in local sandboxed storage
  • Camera & Photo Library Access: Camera used strictly for scanning peer-to-peer settings sync QR codes (no frames recorded or uploaded)
  • Photo Library used solely for parent-selected family card avatars
  • In-App Purchases (Billing): Entitlement token verification processed directly through Apple StoreKit and Google Play Billing for the one-time Full Version Unlock (no financial data accessed or stored)

2. How We Use Collected Information

  • Core App Functionality: Providing the features, tools, and services requested by users.
  • Security & Integrity: Protecting accounts, verifying authorized access, and ensuring data integrity.
  • Service Reliability: Identifying software defects, optimizing performance, and ensuring seamless operation.

3. Data Storage & Security Architecture

🔒 Security Safeguards: We enforce modern security standards. Data in transit is encrypted using TLS 1.3. On-device data is maintained in sandboxed application storage, and cloud databases are protected by authentication controls and access security rules.

4. Third-Party Services & Infrastructure

Little Brain Learners integrates strictly with trusted enterprise infrastructure providers necessary for app delivery:

  • Apple App Store & Google Play Store: Application distribution, family sharing, and automatic software updates
  • Apple StoreKit & Google Play Billing: Secure one-time in-app purchase processing without developer access to payment credentials

We do not sell, rent, or lease personal information to third-party data brokers or marketing networks.

5. Account Deletion & Data Erasure Instructions

In accordance with global privacy standards (including GDPR, CCPA, and Apple App Store / Google Play Store requirements), you possess the absolute right to delete your account, wipe all associated device data, and request complete erasure of personal records.

🗑️ Step-by-Step Account Deletion:
  • No Account Registration Required: Little Brain Learners operates without user accounts, logins, email collections, or passwords
  • On-Device Progress Wipe: You can permanently clear all learning milestone records, custom family member cards, game scores, and settings at any time by clearing application data in device Settings or uninstalling the app
  • Instant Erasure: Because all data is stored strictly on-device in sandboxed storage, removing the app permanently erases 100% of stored records immediately
  • Support Correspondence Erasure: If you have contacted our support team via email, you may email wintbit.dev@gmail.com with the subject line 'Data Deletion Request' to delete your support communication records within 30 days

6. Operational Rules, Restrictions & Immutability

Please note the following operational policies, restrictions, and architectural disclosures governing data handling in Little Brain Learners:

  • Zero Advertising & Behavioral Tracking: No advertising SDKs, behavioral analytics, or third-party tracking identifiers are integrated
  • No Third-Party Data Sharing or Brokerage: We do not sell, rent, monetize, or transfer any user or child data to third parties
  • Fully Offline Operation: All learning modules and memory games run completely on-device without requiring internet access or cloud synchronization
  • Safe Parental Gate: All external links, support emails, and in-app purchase dialogs are secured behind a mathematical parental gate to protect young learners

7. Children's Privacy

Little Brain Learners is designed specifically for early learners and strictly adheres to the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), GDPR-K, Apple Kids Category guidelines (Guidelines 1.3 and 5.1), and Google Play Families Policies. We do not collect, transmit, share, or monetize any personal information from children under 13. All educational gameplay, custom family memory cards, and learning milestones operate 100% offline on the user's local device.

8. Policy Updates & Contact

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. Continued use of Little Brain Learners after revisions indicates acceptance of the updated terms.
