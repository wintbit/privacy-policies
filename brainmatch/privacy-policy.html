BrainMatch Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 02, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how BrainMatch ("we", "our", or "the App") collects, uses, stores, and protects information when you interact with our applications and services.

Our Privacy Commitment: BrainMatch is an interactive memory and cognitive skill-building game. We respect your digital privacy. We do not sell your personal data, nor do we track your activities across external third-party apps for advertising purposes.

1. Information We Collect

To provide reliable and secure functionality for students and players of all ages, BrainMatch collects and processes the following information:

  • Local Game Progress: Scores, completed levels, and achievement badges stored locally on your device
  • Gameplay Diagnostics: Anonymous crash and performance metrics to ensure smooth puzzle execution

2. How We Use Collected Information

  • Core App Functionality: Providing the features, tools, and services requested by users.
  • Security & Integrity: Protecting accounts, verifying authorized access, and ensuring data integrity.
  • Service Reliability: Identifying software defects, optimizing performance, and ensuring seamless operation.

3. Data Storage & Security Architecture

🔒 Security Safeguards: We enforce modern security standards. Data in transit is encrypted using TLS 1.3. On-device data is maintained in sandboxed application storage, and cloud databases are protected by authentication controls and access security rules.

4. Third-Party Services & Infrastructure

BrainMatch integrates strictly with trusted enterprise infrastructure providers necessary for app delivery:

  • Apple Game Center: Optional leaderboards and achievement synchronization
  • App Store: Secure application updates and distribution

We do not sell, rent, or lease personal information to third-party data brokers or marketing networks.

5. User Rights & Data Deletion

In accordance with global privacy standards (including GDPR and CCPA), you possess the right to access, export, rectify, or delete your personal account data.

To request data deletion, contact us at wintbit.dev@gmail.com with the subject line "Data Deletion Request". Requests are processed within 30 days.

6. Children's Privacy

BrainMatch complies with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA). We do not collect, store, or share any personal identifying information from children under 13.

7. Policy Updates & Contact

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. Continued use of BrainMatch after revisions indicates acceptance of the updated terms.
